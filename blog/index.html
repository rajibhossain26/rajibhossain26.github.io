    Who gets left behind when a hurricane is coming?

    Our new paper in the Journal of Transport Geography looks at how gender and rural or urban location shape hurricane evacuation decisions, and how people would get out, among vulnerable residents of the Florida Panhandle.

    Where chronic disease clusters in Georgia

    I mapped diabetes, heart disease and unmet social needs across all 159 Georgia counties. A hot-spot analysis shows two rural belts, a metro-north cold spot, and a few places a simple ranking would miss.
